FAQ
Common questions
What documents can I upload?

Any HR document — employee handbooks, policy PDFs, benefits guides, onboarding docs, org charts. We support PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Google Docs links. Most teams upload 5-15 documents to start.

Will it make up answers?

No. RelyIQ only answers from your uploaded documents. If it can't find the answer in your docs, it says so and escalates to your HR team. Every answer includes a citation to the exact source.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live in under 10 minutes. Upload your docs, customize the greeting, and share the link with your team. No IT involvement needed.

Is our data secure?

Yes. Documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. We don't use your data to train models. Each tenant's data is fully isolated. SOC 2 compliance in progress.

What happens when it can't answer?

RelyIQ escalates to your HR team via email or Slack notification. You see the question, provide the answer, and can optionally add it to your knowledge base for next time.

Can I try it before committing?

Absolutely. We have a live demo you can try right now with sample HR data, plus a 14-day free trial with your own documents. No credit card required.

Does it integrate with Slack or Teams?

Yes. Employees can ask questions directly in Slack or Microsoft Teams without leaving their workflow. Web chat widget is also available for your intranet.
