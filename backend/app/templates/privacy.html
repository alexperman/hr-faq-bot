Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

RelyIQ ("we", "us", "our") operates the RelyIQ platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, company name, and password (stored as a one-way hash). We never store plaintext passwords.

Uploaded Documents

You may upload HR documents (handbooks, policies, benefits guides) to power the AI assistant. These documents are stored encrypted at rest and are only accessible to users within your tenant (organization).

Chat Data

Questions asked by your employees and the AI-generated answers are stored to provide chat history, analytics, and escalation features. This data belongs to your organization.

Usage Data

We automatically collect standard usage information including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. This helps us improve the service and diagnose issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: Process your documents, generate answers, and deliver the chat experience.
  • Improve accuracy: Analyze question patterns to improve answer quality (never using your data to train third-party models).
  • Communicate: Send account-related emails (welcome, billing, security alerts). No marketing emails without explicit opt-in.
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Data Isolation & Multi-Tenancy

Each organization's data is fully isolated at the database level. No tenant can access another tenant's documents, chat history, or user information. Our architecture enforces strict tenant boundaries on every API request.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate RelyIQ:

  • AI Processing (Groq): Your questions are sent to our AI provider to generate answers. We do not send your full documents — only relevant excerpts needed to answer a specific question.
  • Payment Processing (PayPal): Billing information is handled directly by PayPal. We never store credit card numbers.
  • Hosting: Our infrastructure runs on secure cloud providers with SOC 2 compliant data centers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription:

  • Your documents are deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Chat history is deleted within 30 days.
  • Account metadata (email, company name) is retained for 90 days for billing reconciliation, then deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about your organization.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Export: Download your uploaded documents and chat history at any time from the dashboard.
  • Correct: Update your account information through the settings page.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Hashed passwords using bcrypt with per-user salts.
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens.
  • Rate limiting on all API endpoints.
  • Regular security audits and dependency updates.

8. AI & Your Data

We do not use your documents or chat data to train AI models. Your data is used solely to generate answers for your organization. AI-generated answers are clearly marked and always cite the source document.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies only — for authentication (JWT token storage) and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify account owners via email of any material changes at least 14 days before they take effect.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@relyiq.com.
